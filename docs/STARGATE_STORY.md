# Stargate backstory

Author-facing writing reference, updated 6 October 2026. The author accepted the planetary-displacement direction and the supporting corrections discussed with it. This document distinguishes agreement from recommended explanations and remaining design choices. The accepted direction is now reflected in quest prose. This distinguishes implemented writing from Stargate, preservation, astronomical, or ending mechanics, which this revision does not implement.

## Accepted story direction

- The player, or players in multiplayer, worked on the Stargate project before the disaster.
- The experiment attempted to enlarge a delicate connection into a controlled passage. Its cascade expanded beyond the intended boundary and transported Earth into another solar system.
- The transfer worked, but its effects also destroyed the gate. The player cannot immediately operate the wreckage.
- The gate is exceptionally intricate. Reconstruction requires precision, knowledge, and a rebuilt industrial supply chain.
- A long interval separates the catastrophe from awakening. The wasteland develops through prolonged environmental damage and decay.
- The unfamiliar star and unsuitable orbit explain the hostile environment. Meteorite showers are not essential to this explanation or the mystery.
- The endgame goal is to rebuild the gate and bring Earth home, carrying the player's restored land and infrastructure with it.
- The final operation must address the original containment and control failure rather than merely repeat the original test.
- The environment must remain compatible with breathable air, lakes, oceans, cave life, viable seeds, cultures, and restoration. Human civilization collapses; literal extinction of all life is excluded.
- Sarcasm, criticism, and a didactic lesson are optional and should arise from the story if useful.

The earlier time-travel rescue mission, failed return attempt, temporal preservation, and branching-history options are superseded.

## Retained background and writing constraints

The player is an original engineer with fragmented memories, including standing at the gate and waiting. Protected cryogenic survival remains the opening explanation from the author's preceding story. The FTB manual is the player's recovery memo; technical manuals and logs were written by colleagues. The player or players are the only eventual human survivors.

Laboratory machinery is inactive at awakening. Preservation equipment, endurance, and the release mechanism still need an implementation decision. “When the player is born” is currently interpreted as awakening, not literal birth after the catastrophe.

Staff records can cover ordinary work before, during, and after the incident. The six approved incidents remain: moving the supply chest, solving the mechanical bootstrap problem, correcting a reversed pump and adding arrows, testing document retrieval, recording storage concerns, and making a paper electrical backup. Their significance comes from context rather than explicit plot explanations.

Mechanical manual scraps are scattered among toolboxes. Essential identity, cause, and objective information must be discoverable through visible progression; hidden Lost Logs provide optional side evidence.

## Magnetic protection: science and current writing choice

Earth generates its main magnetic field internally. Electrically conducting liquid iron circulates in the outer core, and rotation helps organize the flows sustaining the geodynamo. The Sun does not supply this field. Relocating Earth alone would not stop the core's flow or rotation.

The magnetosphere is the surrounding region where Earth's field interacts with stellar wind. Stronger wind pressure compresses its outer boundary. Storms and changes in the wind's magnetic field can increase disturbances and transfer energy into Earth's magnetic environment without permanently weakening the field generated in the core.

The current quest prose uses the new star's stronger wind compressing and disturbing Earth's magnetosphere while the planet retains its internally generated field. This is the adopted writing explanation; it does not assert a simulated magnetic system. A genuinely weaker internal field would require an additional fictional transfer effect on the geodynamo; no such mechanism has been chosen.

Magnetic protection and ozone protection are different. The field influences charged particles; ozone absorbs much of the harmful ultraviolet radiation. A more active star can increase radiation exposure, and energetic particle events can alter atmospheric chemistry. Do not describe ozone as held in place by magnetism or claim that relocation inevitably strips Earth's atmosphere.

The severity of ecological collapse is a fictional worldbuilding choice, not a calculated consequence of a specified stellar wind. Sustained climate and radiation stress provide the environmental basis while enough air, water, and protected life remain for gameplay.

A disturbed magnetosphere adjusts to its surroundings; that adjustment alone does not justify a very long sleep. The interval explains cumulative ecological collapse, abandonment, weathering, and machinery degradation. It does not make a permanently lethal orbit safe.

## Working chronology

1. **Research:** the team develops a spatial passage using fictional particle-linking physics. Ordinary quantum entanglement does not provide instant communication or transport.
2. **Transfer:** containment fails to limit the cascade's scale. Earth is transported, and the event destroys the gate. The precise rule limiting the cascade remains open.
3. **Aftermath:** the new environment damages climate and ecosystems. People respond and attempt repairs, but survival and lost industry prevent successful reconstruction. Logs record local work and incomplete understanding.
4. **Preservation:** the player leaves a practical recovery memo and survives in protected cryostasis. Colleagues leave manuals and records for a future operator. The preparation date and release trigger remain open.
5. **Long decline:** environmental damage accumulates, civilization disappears, and infrastructure degrades. Some protected records and biological material survive. No exact duration has been set.
6. **Awakening and restoration:** the player recovers knowledge, grows living systems, and rebuilds industry. These activities matter in themselves and make reconstruction possible.
7. **Discovery and return:** evidence establishes displacement. The player rebuilds control and containment for a deliberate planetary transfer into a safe orbit in the home Solar System. Returning Earth permits further recovery; it does not reverse deaths or instantly heal the wasteland.

This chronology supplies an order for writing. Specific astronomical clues, rooms, equipment, and ending effects remain proposals until designed and implemented.

## Narrative and gameplay consequences

Discovery should progress from a ruined laboratory, through evidence of a wider catastrophe and an unfamiliar stellar environment, to the gate's role. Astronomical records and observations are possible clues; their assets and mechanics are not established here.

Technical entries explain actual function. Geography and material entries remain reference writing. Do not turn every machine or material into a Stargate metaphor or repeat the catastrophe in every manual.

Repair should depend on capabilities developed through play: controlled power, precision manufacture, replacement components, and reliable operation. The ending preserves restored land and factories because the planet travels with them.

## Quest prose implemented 6 October 2026

- The awakening entry introduces a recognizable recovery memo and silent equipment.
- The gate entry retains the uncertain waiting memory and identifies unusable remains.
- The precision manual introduces familiar project initials without stating the catastrophe.
- The electrical manual's recovered memo establishes Earth displacement, gate destruction, the player's role, cryogenic preservation, long decline, and returning the planet home.
- Staff logs remain hidden optional records. Their ordinary work details support the main account without being required to understand it.
- Reference and machine entries remain practical. No new quests, tasks, gameplay gates, or ending actions were added.

The memoir and observed astronomical readings are narrative documents, not proof of a changed in-game sky. Detailed implementation choices below remain open. See [quest writing review](QUEST_WRITING_REVIEW.md) and [validation evidence](audit/quest_story_validation.json).

## Remaining consequential decisions

- What limits the original cascade to planetary scale, and how does the rebuilt gate control it?
- How is a safe destination and orbital motion specified relative to the Sun at the time of return?
- How long is the interval, and how do preservation and surviving records fit it?
- What permits awakening while the outside remains harsh but survivable?
- What visible evidence establishes displacement, and how is the return represented in gameplay?

Keep these as design questions. Do not fill them with unapproved rules or carry superseded time-travel restrictions into this story.
