# Quest writing review

Reviewed 3 October 2026; selectively refined 4 October 2026; revised for the accepted story on 6 October 2026. This report covers all 15 chapters, 504 quests, and their titles, subtitles, and descriptions. The comparison baseline is Git commit `9987e78`, which was HEAD when the original review began. Later comparisons retain that baseline.

The current book gives the player more usable information and a clearer distinction between reference notes, instructions, and personal records. The important corrections are factual: where the manual scraps are stored, what machines actually do, and which material properties are merely real-world context. The literary changes aim to give the surviving documents recognizable authors without supplying an invented explanation for the catastrophe.

The original often read faster. Additional text earns its place when it explains a function, prevents an operating mistake, identifies a resource, or establishes a person behind a document. A longer description is not automatically an improvement. The entry-by-entry review below identifies the particular reason for each description and the tradeoffs where they matter.

## Writing decisions

| Part of the book | Voice and intended effect |
| --- | --- |
| Preface | The pack author's direct guidance. Players should understand the challenge and the interface before starting; the original message takes precedence over a more dramatic introduction. |
| Desolate Awakening | Brief observations and practical discovery cues. The player notices the bunker and finds its documents without receiving invented storage locations. |
| Lost Logs | Individual first-person records. Small corrections, frustrations, and preservation choices suggest a working laboratory and people preparing for later readers. |
| Survival Note | Practical recommendations written before long-term survival was demonstrated. Procedures remain clear; uncertainty concerns depleted reserves and future conditions. |
| Environmental Note | Biological functions and limits, with cautious observations about recovery. A productive culture does not establish that the damaged environment will recover. |
| Geological Notes | A field reference: identify the deposit, know where it may occur, and understand the resource it supplies. Relative rarity is more useful than a raw placement calculation. |
| Material Note | Composition, relevant properties, and actual uses. Materials do not all need a lesson or a warning. |
| Processing Note | Explain what a process changes or separates. JEI provides the executable recipe. |
| Kinetic Enlightenment | Mechanical functions and interactions. Explain the mechanism when it helps the player operate or place it. |
| The Pulse of Industry | Storage, flow, distribution, and fluid handling. Descriptions connect the parts of a working network and retain controls that recipes cannot show. |
| Precision Manufacturing | Component roles, processing capabilities, and handling limitations. Fragility warnings keep the user's exact wording. |
| Heavy Industry | Machine roles and industrial capabilities. Larger scale is expressed through actual functions rather than generic claims of advancement. |
| Petroleum Industry | What each chemical or process contributes to the pack. Established chemistry supplies context without becoming an ingredient catalogue. |
| Electrical Engineering | A short introduction to electrical supply and machinery. Its recovered memo also carries the late story reveal; it does not claim a playable planetary transfer. |
| Bioengineering | Culture production and its uses, followed by one specific uncertainty about survival outside a sealed plate. |

Different voices are strongest where the documents already imply different speakers. Reference entries remain straightforward; forcing a dramatic clue into each material would make the archive less believable. Technical chapters necessarily share vocabulary, so variation comes from selecting the relevant fact rather than replacing clear terms with synonyms.

## Concrete refinements and their reasons

- **Manual scraps:** all three are described as being in laboratory toolboxes. The structure contains the top scrap in a gray toolbox at `[34,29,28]`, and the middle and bottom scraps in another at `[40,25,29]`. The prose no longer directs the player toward a suitcase, shelf, dark corner, or separate storage place.
- **Discovery labels:** “Rocks and Materials” identifies the actual document subject. “The Reference Shelf” suggested a physical place that had not been established.
- **Function over recipe narration:** descriptions explain effects, transfer, separation, control, or component roles. Ingredient quantities and manufacturing sequences remain in JEI. Necessary placement and interaction instructions stay in the book.
- **Heavy machine core:** removed the repeated comparison of oil amounts from the core entry. Its dedicated petroleum note retains the deliberate lubricant comparison, with quantities left to JEI; the core description focuses on why oil belongs in the assembly.
- **Precision entries:** merged repeated brass properties, removed generic equipment and control-chip conclusions, and clarified the arm base's relationship to a completed arm. Each now stops after its useful distinction.
- **Integrated electron tube:** the module is explained through the actual detection, signaling, and coordinated movement of machines built with it. The final pass removes unsupported claims about multistage circuitry inside the item.
- **Brevity and narrative:** simple stock entries stop after identification and useful applications. Repeated replacement-tool advice is concentrated in Survival Tools. The backpack's familiar pocket order is left uninterpreted; the precision and electrical logs now record retrieval testing and a paper backup without a shared comic ending.
- **Supported construction details:** removed the backpack's unsupported iron-frame description and the hammer's unsupported repair claim. A plausible explanation is insufficient when it implies a feature the pack does not provide.
- **Material scope:** resin is described through its binder and rubber-feedstock uses; zinc's returned catalyst role is distinguished from its real-world use in protective coatings. The latter does not imply an implemented coating mechanic.
- **Biological scope:** removed generic storage directions and an unnecessary record-keeping assignment. The remaining culture limitations explain whether an active sample can propagate and why successful incubation does not prove field persistence.
- **Language and formatting:** corrected fragments and awkward phrasing, made burner controls consistent with the original dashed cyan banners, and completed missing color resets.
- **Warnings:** all five precision dropping warnings remain exactly `&cHandle it carefully, could be factured if it falls on the ground.&r`. The spelling is retained because the user explicitly protected this wording; a warning is separate from a control tooltip.

Representative evidence is in the [starter bunker structure](/Users/ivan/Documents/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/CWI/kubejs/data/cwi/structures/starter_bunker.nbt), the [Petzite mapping](/Users/ivan/Documents/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/CWI/kubejs/startup_scripts/Preload/Global.js:218), the [zinc-assisted reaction](/Users/ivan/Documents/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/CWI/kubejs/server_scripts/Recipes/Chemistry.js:629), and the [incubator recipes](/Users/ivan/Documents/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/CWI/kubejs/server_scripts/Multiblocked2/Incubator.js:1). The [brass document packet](/Users/ivan/Documents/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/CWI/kubejs/server_scripts/Progression/MechanicalAge.js:925) and [steel document packet](/Users/ivan/Documents/curseforge/minecraft/Instances/CWI/kubejs/server_scripts/Progression/IndustrialAge.js:584) support the preservation theme in the logs.

The personal logs are authored narrative scenes approved by the pack author, grounded in these operations and intended as optional clues to the backstory. For example, the cleaner's named preparation and observation rooms create a work setting; they are not verified room labels or navigation instructions. The logs suggest concern for later readers without identifying the catastrophe's cause or promising a particular ending. Their authors can record events before, during, or after the incident, but the player or players are the only eventual survivors.

## Requirements used for this review

1. Review descriptions, titles, and subtitles throughout every chapter.
2. Preserve IDs, dependencies, tasks, rewards, and layout unless a structural correction is authorized.
3. Preserve the original intent and important details, especially in the preface.
4. Check gameplay claims against scripts, configuration, generation, and installed implementations; do not describe unimplemented effects.
5. Use established real-world facts suitable for a short explanation. Do not present them as causes of gameplay effects the pack does not implement.
6. Explain construction only when it clarifies a real function, limit, or deliberate recipe relationship. Omit speculative engineering explanations.
7. One purpose is sufficient: instruction, identification, observation, atmosphere, character, or narrative progression.
8. Leave ingredient inventories, quantities, patterns, assembly sequences, fuel tables, and recipe probabilities to JEI. Explain process principles when useful.
9. Retain useful controls, placement, important limitations, and interactions that JEI does not adequately explain.
10. Keep material entries about identity, composition, relevant properties, facts, and uses.
11. Keep geography entries about ores, stones, deposits, biomes, occurrence, heights, rarity, associated resources, and uses.
12. Present practical occurrence information without raw placement counts or implied guaranteed finds.
13. Select facts deliberately; avoid exhaustive trivia and instructional or scientific padding.
14. Vary voice naturally by subject, document, and author; avoid a rigid paragraph template.
15. Let earlier authors express specific uncertainty about future survival and recovery. Established controls and machine behavior remain dependable.
16. Keep narrative clues occasional, restrained, and consistent with the story. Do not invent unresolved causes, identities, destinations, or endings.
17. Preserve centered cyan controls between matching dashed lines. Keep warnings distinct and preserve protected wording and presentation.
18. Remove useless labels, name restatements, and generic repeated advice. Preserve useful atmosphere, humor, and character; let substance determine length.
19. Check coverage, facts, formatting, protected text, SNBT, and quest structure. Report limits and any conflict with protected wording.

## Story revision implemented 6 October 2026

All 504 quests in all 15 chapters were reviewed against the accepted planetary-displacement story and the existing writing requirements. Forty-seven entries changed. Review coverage comprised 39 opening/preface/log/electrical entries, 212 survival/environment/geology/material/bioengineering entries, 111 processing/kinetic/fluid entries, and 142 precision/heavy-industry/petroleum entries.

The opening recognizes the player's own recovery memo without supplying the full backstory. The unusable gate and precision manual's familiar initials lead toward the electrical packet. Its normal manual-item route reveals that the test moved Earth, destroyed the gate, and left the player with a reconstruction mission after prolonged cryogenic preservation. The goal is returning the entire planet to a safe orbit around its own Sun, preserving the player's restored land and machinery. Returning does not undo deaths or instantly repair ecosystems.

The magnetic explanation in the prose uses stronger stellar wind compressing and disturbing Earth's magnetosphere while the planet continues generating its own field. It does not make ordinary quantum entanglement a transport mechanism or assert that relocation stops the geodynamo. Meteorite showers and the superseded time-travel rescue are absent from the explanation.

The hidden staff records retain their six approved workplace incidents. Added observations, failed checks, retained corrections, supply decisions, and shutdown notes let readers make connections without a log explaining the whole transfer. A second editorial pass removed claims of dated evidence where no dates were shown and removed directions explaining how to read the literary clues. The main reveal explicitly connects preservation to the long decline.

Technical entries remain about actual function, controls, limitations, and useful scientific context. The factual updates include Fermento's new dried-sample incubation route, the distinction between revival and propagation, lead's inventory Neurotoxin limitation, and TFMG versus Create Diesel Generators gasoline. Natural mineral colors are identified as real specimens rather than pack appearances. Recipe catalogues and unsupported construction or operating claims were shortened.

The [retained validation evidence](audit/quest_story_validation.json) records every changed ID, its before/after text, and rationale. All 569 tasks and non-presentation data are identical to the snapshot; IDs, dependencies, rewards, layout, hidden archive settings, five exact precision warnings, and 23 control banners are preserved. Non-chapter quest configuration is byte-identical. The installed FTB parser accepts all 15 chapters and 2,066 presentation strings; the formatting check accepts 1,870 codes. In-game wrapping, centering, and page balance have not been reviewed.

The appendix below has updated rationales for the 47 changed entries and refreshed source links throughout. Remaining transfer bounds, orbital targeting details, preservation duration/release, and ending implementation are design questions, not invented facts in these quests.
